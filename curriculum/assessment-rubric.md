# Robotics Learning Assessment Rubric

Use this rubric at the end of each phase and during the weekly review.

## Scoring
| Area | Weight | What good evidence looks like |
|---|---:|---|
| Theory and conceptual understanding | 20% | Can explain assumptions, equations, coordinate frames, and limitations |
| Implementation | 30% | Working code/configuration with clear structure and reproducible steps |
| Simulation and experiments | 25% | Valid experiment, appropriate scenarios, and recorded results |
| Debugging and diagnosis | 15% | Can isolate a fault, explain its cause, and verify the fix |
| Documentation | 10% | Clear README, diagrams, commands, results, and lessons learned |

**Suggested checkpoint:** 75%. A lower score is a signal to revisit the weakest area before building more complexity, not a reason to rush.

## Phase gate
Before moving to the next phase, confirm:
- [ ] I can explain the core concepts without relying entirely on notes.
- [ ] I have completed the practical exercise.
- [ ] I can run the result from a clean terminal/session using documented steps.
- [ ] I tested at least one edge case or failure condition.
- [ ] I recorded what did not work and how I diagnosed it.
- [ ] I committed the work to Git.

## Research paper reading notes
For each paper, record:
1. Problem being addressed
2. Main contribution
3. Assumptions and required inputs
4. Method overview
5. Datasets and evaluation metrics
6. Strengths and limitations
7. What could be reproduced in a small experiment
8. Relevance to robot perception or autonomous systems

## Weekly review questions
- What did I study?
- What did I implement or simulate?
- What worked, and what did not?
- What concept remains unclear?
- What is the most important blocker?
- What single measurable deliverable will I complete next week?
